Required Redline = COGS + overhead burden at that volume + Helio Margin. Every row is different because overhead/W shrinks as volume grows.
GM $ = (Helio Margin + overhead burden) × kW × 1,000 — total dollars above COGS.
GM % = (Redline − COGS) ÷ Redline.
