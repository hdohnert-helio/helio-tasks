Redline $/W = COGS + your target adder. This is the floor you hand to sales — nothing goes below it.
GM $ = adder × monthly kW × 1,000 — total gross margin dollars at that volume.
GM % = (Redline − COGS) ÷ Redline — margin as a percent of revenue.
OH covered? shows whether your adder is enough to cover fixed overhead at that volume. Red means you need more kW or a higher adder.
